# Code of Conduct

lvdsim is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want working on it to
be a good experience for everyone, so we ask for a few simple things.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be constructive. Say what you would change and why. Report problems with enough detail that someone can act
  on them.
- Welcome newcomers. Answer questions kindly, and say so when you do not know.
- Give credit. Name the source of an idea or a piece of code, and respect the licences of other people's work.
- Accept decisions. The maintainers decide what goes into the project; they will explain their reasons when asked.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualised language or attention, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption of discussions, and spam.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every space of the project: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and to
people who represent the project in public. It also applies to private messages sent because of the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for applying this code. They may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues
and pull requests, and block people who do not follow it. They will try to talk first when the matter is small,
and will act at once when it is serious.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting or contact the maintainers listed in the repository
settings. Reports are handled in confidence, and the people involved are not told who reported unless that is
needed to resolve the matter. A maintainer who is part of the problem steps aside, and another maintainer decides.
